Check the private-development stack:

  kubectl --context kraken -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods
  kubectl --context kraken -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deployment/{{ include "noah.fullname" . }}

The Kraken SmartBus SQS and S3 resources are intentionally not consumed by
Noah. They remain available to the Splunk workloads through their runtime
ServiceAccount.
